50+ Years of Heritage

Founded by Murugesh in 1970

Murugesh Maligai was established in 1970 by Mr. Murugesh. For over five decades and across three generations, we have been serving families in Annur and Coimbatore with wholesome food, pure provisions, and uncompromised integrity.

Our core mission has always remained simple: to make every customer happy by offering daily household necessities at honest wholesale rates, sourced directly from trusted millers and local farmers.

🌿 Zero-Plastic Carry Bag Policy: To protect our environment and future generations, polythene carry-bags are strictly prohibited in our store. We warmly invite our customers to bring their own reusable cloth bags and join us in our green journey.

"Our main goal is to make every customer happy, providing quality products at the most reasonable rates, so they leave with a whole heart."
— Founder Murugesh
